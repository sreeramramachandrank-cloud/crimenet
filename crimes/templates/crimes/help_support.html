❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1 How do I register a new FIR in the system?
Navigate to New FIR Registration from the main dashboard. Fill in all required fields including complainant details, incident information, and crime category. After completing the form, review the information and submit. You can then capture biometric data and attach evidence documents.
2 Who can access the Crime Analytics dashboard?
Crime Analytics features are restricted to authorized police officers with investigator or administrator roles. Access permissions are granted based on rank and departmental requirements. Contact your system administrator if you require access to analytics features.
3 How do I print an official FIR document?
Open the specific FIR from the FIR Management section, review the details, and click the Print FIR button located at the top-right of the page. The system will generate a formatted PDF document suitable for official use and legal proceedings.
4 How can I update the status of an ongoing investigation?
Access the FIR details page, locate the Status Update section, select the appropriate status from the dropdown menu (Under Investigation, Closed, Court Case, etc.), add relevant notes or remarks, and click Save Changes. All status updates are logged with timestamps for audit purposes.
5 What should I do if I encounter a system error?
Take a screenshot of the error message, note the time and action you were performing, and contact technical support immediately via email or phone. For critical errors affecting operations, use the emergency helpline. Include your user ID and station details when reporting issues.
6 How do I search for existing criminal records?
Navigate to the Criminal Database section and use the search filters. You can search by Aadhaar number for unique identification, name or alias for text-based searches, or physical characteristics. The system also supports biometric matching for fingerprint identification.
7 Is training available for new users?
Yes, comprehensive training programs are available. Access online training modules through the training portal, attend scheduled in-person training sessions at your regional training center, or watch video tutorials available in the Resources section of the dashboard.
8 How is my data secured in the system?
CrimeNET employs bank-level encryption for data storage and transmission, role-based access controls to limit data visibility, comprehensive audit trails tracking all system activities, and regular security audits. All data is backed up daily and stored in secure, redundant locations.
